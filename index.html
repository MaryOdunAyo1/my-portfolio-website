How I Work

The process behind every system I build.

1. Understand the real problem

Before touching any tool, I get clear on what's actually costing time or getting missed, not just what a client or user says they want, but the problem underneath it.

2. Map the workflow end to end

I sketch the full path data needs to travel: where it enters, what needs to happen to it, and where it needs to land, before picking a single tool.

3. Build and connect the system

I wire together the interface, automation platform, and AI layer needed to move data through that path automatically, testing each connection as I go.

4. Test with real inputs, then refine

I run real, messy, unpredictable inputs through the system, not just clean examples, and adjust the logic until it holds up.

1. AI Chat Assistant for Telegram

The Problem

Most AI chat tools live in their own separate app, forcing people to switch away from where their conversations already happen.

What I Built

A ChatGPT-style AI assistant built directly into Telegram, so people can chat with an AI without leaving the app they already use daily.

Telegram Bot > Make.com > Google Gemini AI > Reply sent back to Telegram

How It Works

  • Telegram Bot API handles the user-facing chat interface
  • Make.com manages incoming messages and the response flow
  • Google Gemini AI generates each reply in real time

Result

A fully working AI chatbot inside Telegram, with no separate app and no extra login. Responses come back instantly, with no lag compared to switching to a separate AI app.

2. Automated Email Triage & Summarization System

The Problem

Important emails get buried in inbox noise, and reading everything in full doesn't scale.

What I Built

Monitors an inbox, filters for what actually matters, summarizes it with AI, and automatically archives only the important emails.

Gmail > Make.com > Google Gemini AI (summarize) > Importance filter > Google Docs

How It Works

  • Make.com watches Gmail for new incoming messages
  • Each email is summarized by Google Gemini AI
  • A filter decides whether the email is actually important
  • Only qualifying summaries get logged to Google Docs

Result

A running, organized log of only what matters, cutting inbox reading time down to a quick scan, an estimated 70 to 80% reduction versus reading every email in full.

3. Automated Sales & Expense Logging Bot

The Problem

Manual, end-of-day spreadsheet entry is slow, easy to skip, and error-prone for a salesperson on the move.

What I Built

Lets a salesperson log expenses and sales as a casual chat message, with AI turning that free text into clean spreadsheet rows.

Telegram Bot > Make.com > Google Gemini AI (extract) > JSON Parse > Google Sheets > Confirmation back to Telegram

How It Works

  • Telegram bot accepts free-text entries, no fixed format required
  • Google Gemini AI extracts structured data (item, amount, category)
  • JSON Parse breaks that output into fields Make.com can map
  • Parsed fields are written straight into Google Sheets
  • A confirmation message is sent back to the user in Telegram

Result

A casual chat message becomes a clean spreadsheet row instantly, turning what used to be 5 to 10 minutes of manual entry into a single message, with zero manual formatting.

4. AI Lead Qualification & Routing System

The Problem

Businesses often receive multiple enquiries but have no consistent way to identify which leads actually deserve immediate attention.

What I Built

An AI-powered lead intake system that evaluates potential customers, scores them, and automatically routes high and medium priority leads to the right notifications, with no manual sorting required.

Form > n8n > Google Gemini > Lead Score > Priority Routing > Google Sheets + Gmail

How It Works

  • Customer submits an enquiry through a form
  • Google Gemini analyzes the business problem, buying intent, budget, timeline, and potential value
  • The AI generates a lead score (0 to 100) and assigns a priority: HIGH, MEDIUM, or LOW
  • n8n's conditional logic routes the lead based on its priority
  • The lead is recorded in Google Sheets
  • Gmail automatically sends the appropriate notification for high- and medium-priority leads

Result

A working automated lead qualification and routing workflow that turns a customer enquiry into a structured, prioritized lead, removing the need for manual lead review entirely.
